01 Engineering course

P1

School project · Semester 4 · Team of 4 · ~80h/person

How to detect AI generated images in order to protect oneself from them ?

AI generated images are everywhere. To understand how a image can be generated by AI and how difficult it is to find a generated image, we have done a lot of research. During this project, we try to see regular patterns after applying Fourier transform on images to see AI generated images.

Key skills

  • Teamwork — Organization (google docs), communication (Whatsapp), report writing.
  • Ecological and Societal Impact — AI generated images influence us, electric and water problems .
  • Algorithm Implementation — Python.

Reflection

We create a program to see regular pattern but it was not pertinent : we see nothing. The subject was too recent to have result with our level but we learn a lot of things, we have a better comprehension of the subject.
